# Titan Omega Infinity — Commercial Playbook (2026-07-06)

The honest, compressed version of the 12-phase protocol. Everything a number
here is an estimate and labelled as such; revenue to date from Titan: **$0**.

## Phase 1 — What this product REALLY is

A **single-operator autonomous business cockpit**: FastAPI backend + 3D Next.js
front end, 102 scripted-persona agents, multi-provider LLM chain with failover,
content/job/finance engines, Telegram control, Make.com ingestion. Strengths:
spectacular UI, real deploy pipeline, honest ledgers, provider-agnostic LLM
layer. Weaknesses vs. commercial SaaS: single-tenant (one founder per deploy),
in-memory store with JSON persistence (not Postgres), no billing, no team
accounts, no SSO/audit logs. That is *fine* — see strategy below.

**Ideal customer (validated by market behavior, not wishes):**
1. Agencies/freelancer-served SMBs who want "an AI ops dashboard" *deployed for
   them* — they buy outcomes, not repos.
2. Indie founders/devs who buy premium boilerplates ($49–$299) to skip months
   of work.
3. (Later) SMB teams paying $29–99/mo — requires multi-tenant rework.

**Why someone pays:** the 30-second demo wow + "this is deployable today" +
source access. **Why they don't:** single-tenant, unknown brand, no reviews yet.

## Phase 2/3 — Monetization scored (expected value for a solo, $0-capital founder)

| Model | Time to 1st sale | Difficulty | Realistic 90-day range | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Custom deployment / white-label service (Upwork + direct) | 1–4 weeks | Low — sell what exists | $300–$3,000 | ⭐ DO FIRST |
| Founder/Agency lifetime license (boilerplate, via Dodo) | 4–10 weeks (needs audience) | Medium | $0–$1,500 | DO SECOND |
| SaaS subscription | 2–4 months build first | High (multi-tenant, billing) | $0 early | LATER |
| Enterprise ($5k+/yr, SSO/audit/RBAC) | 6+ months, needs logos | High | $0 early | ROADMAP |
| Marketplace templates/plugins | after license base exists | Medium | small | LATER |

**Strategy: sell the SERVICE first (Titan as proof + starting template), the
LICENSE second, SaaS only after real demand.** This is why the demo Space and
LinkedIn/Upwork presence matter more right now than SSO or installers.

## Phase 5/6 — Positioning + pricing

- **Tagline:** "Your business, running itself — watch it think."
- **Elevator pitch:** Titan Omega Infinity is an autonomous AI business OS: a
  cinematic 3D command center where AI agents debate strategy, produce content,
  track money, and find work — deployable for your business in days, not months.
- **Pricing (introductory → later):**
  - Founder License: **$99 → $199** (single business, source, updates 1yr)
  - Agency License: **$399 → $799** (client deploys, white-label)
  - Deploy-for-you service: **$300–$1,500/project** (scope-based, via Upwork
    first for reviews, then direct)
  - Enterprise: "contact" — do not publish a number until one exists.

**First launch platform: LinkedIn** (organic proof-of-work posts + DMs to
agencies), because the buyer of the *service* lives there and it costs $0.
Product Hunt comes after a checkout exists.

**Honest expectations:** first service revenue is realistically **1–4 weeks**
away with daily outreach; first license sale typically needs **300–1,000
engaged followers or ~30 warm conversations**. "Millions" is not a plan — a
believable path is $500–$2k/mo within 3–6 months from services + licenses,
compounding from there IF distribution is worked daily. Anyone promising more
without an audience is lying to you.

**30-day plan:** Week 1: demo live, LinkedIn 3 posts + reel, Upwork live, 10
DMs/day. Week 2: Dodo checkout + license page, keep posting, first free-deploy
testimonial. Week 3: case study post, raise Upwork rate, 2nd testimonial.
Week 4: license launch post + Product Hunt prep. Every week: ship one visible
improvement, post the clip.
